Our Story

Made because a fixed route shouldn't ignore the season

We're a small, independent safari operator — not a large tour company. Here's the plain version of how we got here.

Where It Started

A route that missed everything happening nearby

Safari Wind Ivy started with a specific frustration: a fixed safari itinerary that followed the same route regardless of season, missing significant wildlife activity happening just outside its planned path that particular week. We started checking daily activity reports and adjusting routes around them instead.

Every trip since has been planned around that same responsive approach.

How We Work

A few habits we hold to on every trip

01

Daily Activity Checks

Guides track wildlife activity daily, not from a seasonal average.

02

Small Groups

A limited group size so every guest gets a real, unobstructed view.

03

Local Guides

Guides based locally, not flown in for the season.

04

Flexible Routes

Routes adjusted mid-trip when activity shifts.
