Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Safari Wind Ivy ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information gathered through this website and direct contact for trip planning. We are a small, independently operated safari planner, and this policy applies only to this site and our own direct communications.

Information We Collect

When you use the contact form, we collect the information you choose to provide — name, email, and your message, which may include trip planning details. We do not require an account or payment details through this website.

How We Use Information

Information submitted through our contact form is used solely to respond to your trip inquiry. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Analytics and the Google Tag

This site uses Google's tagging service (gtag.js) to understand overall site traffic in aggregate.

Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain reasonable business records.
